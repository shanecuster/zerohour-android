- New repeat: the Nth weekday of a month, for holidays like Thanksgiving (4th Thursday of November), the last Monday of May, or the 1st Friday of every month.
- A task with a due time is no longer late when the time passes, only once its day ends.
- New colors: a soft red for Events, kept apart from the overdue red, and a title banner that fades to each theme's orange.
- A new app icon: a green hourglass with peach sand.
