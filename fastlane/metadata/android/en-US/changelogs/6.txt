- Events can run for days: give one an end date (like Dec 1 to Dec 24) and get a reminder every day of the range, with Stop for this year, and Keep or Remove on the last day.
- Running events are listed first, with the days left.
- Repeating tasks can stop on an "Until" date.
- The calendar won't pick an end before the start.
- Items with end dates sync in a newer format: your other devices need this release of zerohour to show them.
